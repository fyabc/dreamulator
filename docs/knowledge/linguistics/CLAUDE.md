# 语言学知识库

本目录存放语言学（linguistics）参考文档，为架空世界的语言与文字设计提供科学依据。

## 已有文档

- `language-design-guide.md` — 架空语言与文字设计指南（语言/文字分类、音系学、语序类型学、核心词表、历时演化、纯表意系统）

## 规划中的文档

- `phonetics_and_phonology.md` — 语音学与音系学（IPA 音位、发音特征、音节结构）
- `grammar_typology.md` — 语法类型学（形态学类型、格系统、时态/体/语气）
- `writing_system_evolution.md` — 文字系统演化史（古文字学方法、介质影响、隶变机制）

## 关键参考

- Swadesh, M. (1952). "Lexico-statistic dating of prehistoric ethnic contacts." *Proceedings of the American Philosophical Society*, 96(4), 452–463.
- Dolgopolsky, A. B. (1964). "A probabilistic hypothesis concerning the oldest relationships among the language families of northern Eurasia." In *Typology, Relationship and Time*.
- Wierzbicka, A. (1996). *Semantics: Primes and Universals*. Oxford University Press.
- Greenberg, J. H. (1963). "Some universals of grammar with particular reference to the order of meaningful elements." In *Universals of Language*, MIT Press.
- Crystal, D. (2010). *The Cambridge Encyclopedia of Language*, 3rd ed. Cambridge University Press.
